# Gilmore Cleaning Services

Booking web app for Gilmore Cleaning Services: lawn care, house cleaning and moving, gutters, windows, car detailing, pressure washing, trash can sanitization and pet waste removal.

## What's here

- `index.html` is the whole app (HTML, CSS and JavaScript in one file). Open it in a browser to run it.
- `img/` holds the service photos from the Gilmore Instagram posts.

## How booking works today

Customers pick a service, see open times (Mon–Sat, 8:00 AM–6:00 PM, blocked by each service's length), enter contact details and confirm. They can reschedule or cancel from My appointments.

Appointments are saved only in the customer's own browser. Nothing reaches the business yet.

## To take real bookings

1. A backend and database so every booking reaches one place and two customers can't take the same slot.
2. Notifications (text or email) to the business and the customer on book, reschedule and cancel.
3. Optional: sync with the owner's calendar, online deposits or payments, and hosting on a custom domain.
